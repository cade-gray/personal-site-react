What changed from the current site

Kept The wave motif — now one continuous SVG layer rather than a tiled background, so every line runs unbroken across the page. 1.5px stroke, 220px period, 12px amplitude, rows 22px apart, 20s drift. The favicon reuses the same sine maths at icon scale, behind a CG monogram taken from the Dimitri Swank outlines. Plus #121212, #00cf94, #ebebeb, Comfortaa, Dimitri Swank, and every link on the site.
Retired The 10px offset accent drop-shadow and 2rem radii. Depth now comes from hairline borders, a raised surface, and one soft accent glow.
Reframed The hero leads with a 26px statement rather than a display headline — there is no h1 on screen, so the page carries a visually-hidden one. Dimitri Swank is the wordmark and signature only.
Added A translucent accent tint bookends the page at header and footer, and the four brand-colored social blocks became neutral tiles that light up on hover — so the accent stays the one thing that draws the eye.
